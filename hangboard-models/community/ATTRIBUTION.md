## beastmaker-1000-stl-cc-by-sa/

- **Beastmaker 1000 Hangboard** by Lincoln Potochnick (@LincolnPotoch_666075),
  <https://www.printables.com/model/696461-beastmaker-1000-hangboard>, licence
  **CC BY-SA 4.0** (<https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/>).
- Remix of **Beastmaker 1000** by NBianconi, <https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:6319676>,
  licence **CC BY-SA** (as declared on the Printables remix page).
- Three STL parts (each about 205 × 150 × 58 mm) that dovetail into a 3D-printable copy of
  the 1000. Split for print beds; join them at x = 205 and 410 mm to compare with
  `../../beastmaker-1000/`.
- Files: `beastmaker_1000_intera-part-1.stl`, `-part-2.stl`, `-part-3.stl` and the
  Printables PDF summary sheet.
