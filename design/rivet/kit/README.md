# Rivet asset kit

Generated by `design/rivet/tools/kit.py`; edit the brand sources, not these files.

- `logo/`: symbol (Night, Snow, Glow), wordmark, horizontal and stacked lockups (Night, Snow), the
  Snow lockup with a Glow symbol for Night grounds, the signature (lockup and line) and the app icon.
  SVG and PDF masters are cropped tight; the PNGs carry the clear space (one bar height on every side).
  The wordmark is artwork: place these files, never retype it.
- `tokens/`: one source in five formats. `rivet.tokens.json` (colours, scales, light and dark roles
  including raised, track and the five timer states (Get ready, Hang, Rest, Recover, Done), the type scale with the timer, radii, motion
  with the 8 px lift, the Glow rule), `rivet.css`, `tailwind.rivet.js` (Tailwind / NativeWind),
  `RivetTokens.swift` (SwiftUI) and `RivetTokens.kt` (Jetpack Compose). Glow has no tint scale on purpose.
- `templates/`: business card front and back (85 × 55 mm PDF and PNG), social post 1080 × 1080, story
  1080 × 1920 and two App Store screenshots 1290 × 2796. The editable versions are on the Penpot page
  **05 Templates**. Social and story PNGs embed placeholder photos and are not committed.
- `icons/`: the custom hangboard and spray-board icons, Regular and Fill, on Phosphor's 256 grid.
- `fonts/`: Nunito 300–900 (SIL Open Font License).
- `posters/`: the ten posters as CMYK print PDFs at 50 × 70 cm plus 1200 px previews (not committed:
  they embed placeholder photos, which are also too small for print; replace them with the shoot).
- `motion/`: loading and startup animations and the brand film (`film.html`, preview `rivet-film.webp`).

Glow is the colour of the hang. It fills the Hang screen and the app icon, and colours the symbol on
Night. Nowhere else.
